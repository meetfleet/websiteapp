Before you submit

The fastest way to delete your account is in the app: Settings → Delete Account. It takes effect immediately. Use this form only if you can no longer sign in, or if you deleted the app without deleting your account.

Once your request is verified, we permanently delete or irreversibly anonymize:

  • Your profile, photos, and identity details;
  • Your messages, pokes, plans, and activity history;
  • Your location, device, and usage data.

This is permanent and irreversible. We cannot restore a deleted account. We may retain a limited record where the law requires it, for fraud prevention, or to resolve a dispute — see our Privacy Policy. Backup copies may persist for a short period before being overwritten.
